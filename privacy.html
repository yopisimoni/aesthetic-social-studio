Privacy notice

How we use your information

Aesthetic Social Studio uses information you submit through the website only to respond to trial enquiries, provide requested services, manage client relationships, or send Clinic Content Notes when you have explicitly subscribed.

Information we may collect

This may include your name, clinic name, email address, website or social profile, project notes, and newsletter consent status.

Why we use it

Trial-enquiry information is used to respond to your request and manage the proposed project. Newsletter information is used only to send Clinic Content Notes and manage your subscription.

Sharing and storage

Website form submissions are processed through FormSubmit and delivered to the Aesthetic Social Studio inbox. Information may also be recorded in our internal lead tracker for business administration. We do not sell subscriber or enquiry data.

Newsletter choices

You can unsubscribe from Clinic Content Notes at any time by replying to a newsletter email with “unsubscribe” or by emailing aestheticsocialstudio.co@gmail.com with the subject “Unsubscribe”. We will remove you from future newsletter sends.

Your requests

If you want us to correct or delete information you submitted, contact aestheticsocialstudio.co@gmail.com.
